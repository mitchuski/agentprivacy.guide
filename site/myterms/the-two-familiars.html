⚔️🧙 The Two Familiars

The MyTerms docs are the public-facing claim set; alongside them live two browser-extension experiments — the dual-agent pair, held apart so neither can betray the other (I(X;Y_S,Y_M) = I(X;Y_S) + I(X;Y_M)):

⚔️ The Swordsman

The agreement layer familiar — the IEEE 7012 Individual's Agent, Proposer, Recorder, and Auditor roles. Proffers, negotiates, signs, records, and returns to verify. Spells: SLASH (assert boundary) and WARD (gate). Lives in its own browser process — separate storage, separate permissions. Local experiments: swordsman/, swordsman-blade/, myswordsman/.

🧙 The Mage

The delegation layer familiar — the Proposer that deep-scans the page and proposes which SD-BASE term fits, operating inside the Σ-scope of agreements the Swordsman signs — and never enforces. Spells: SCAN and INSCRIBE. It carries the spellbook; it cannot render. Local experiments: mage/, mages-spell/.

⿻ The Gap

The Customer Commons registry, the constellation-hash, and the bilateral record. The held-apart separation is the security model. The extensions are experiments; if they and the docs disagree, the docs win — they are the public claim set.
