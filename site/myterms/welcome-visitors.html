⚔️📜✍️🔐 MyTerms — The Agreement Layer

MyTerms is IEEE Std 7012™-2025 — machine-readable personal privacy terms, hosted neutrally by Customer Commons (customercommons.org/p7012). The terms are written by persons — not imposed, but proposed: the First Person proposes, the entity accepts, counter-offers once, or declines. This site carries agentprivacy's MyTerms body of work: the founding-member application to the MyTerms Alliance and the IEEE 7012 integration into the dual-agent architecture.

The honest line, always: the standard specifies agreement, not enforcement.

Where it sits in the architecture

In the privacy value equation Φ = Φ_agent(Σ) · Φ_data(Δ) · Φ_inference(Γ), IEEE 7012 is the Σ axis (agent separation) with coarse Δ policy — and it is silent on Γ. Compliance is a precondition for measurable Σ, not a substitute for the dual-agent architecture or the cryptographic substrate beneath it. The standard names what the contract is; agentprivacy supplies the enforcement the standard does not.
