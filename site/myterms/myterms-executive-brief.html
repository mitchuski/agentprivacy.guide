MyTerms Alliance — Executive Brief

From: privacymage / agentprivacy.ai
To: MyTerms Alliance / Customer Commons
Date: February 1, 2026 (refreshed April 22, 2026 against IEEE 7012 Integration Plan V2)

The Ask

agentprivacy requests founding membership in the MyTerms Alliance, bringing implementation capacity, community energy, and sustainable development to support IEEE 7012 adoption.

Who We Are

agentprivacy is a dual-agent protocol architecture. We're building the Swordsman browser agent, which implements IEEE 7012-2025 as the agreement layer — the bilateral protocol on which enforcement is built, not the enforcement itself. Not a wallet. Not an identity system. The Swordsman implements the agreement; the Mage operates inside it; together they produce the agent separation that the standard alone does not require. The protocol layer between protection and delegation is a communication substrate that every community can extend with their own secret language while maintaining interoperability. Privacy Value Model V5.4 scores the three axes — agent separation (Σ), data (Δ), and inference (Γ) — multiplicatively, so architectural shortcuts are visible rather than averaged away.

What We Bring

  • Implementation — Production Swordsman browser agent implementing the P7012 agreement protocol, MRPAZ headers, bilateral chronicle records (§5.2.4), and visual enforcement feedback. The Σ separation, Δ policy, and Γ-aware scoring live in the implementation, not in the standard.
  • Diffusion — Weekly live coding streams integrating MyTerms into real sites with real guests. Named venues ahead: AIW #3 (agent-architecture audience; Σ mapping and the "agreement, not enforcement" clarification) and IIW #43 (vouchable-credential / VRC crosswalk). Privacy is Value V5 blog series Parts 3–5 carry the narrative.
  • Narrative — Five Spellbooks of living documentation, unified by the Grimoire JSON (v10.1.0). IEEE 7012 is referenced as ambient agreement infrastructure; when the Second Person Spellbook opens, "The Two Parties" grounds the book in the standard's bilateral primitive.
  • Architecture — Guild-based secret languages on a shared waist (IEEE 7012 + W3C DPV + ODRL). Each community's vocabulary is locally rich and globally interoperable, consistent with ISO 23903 reconciliation (Annex D).
  • Sustainability — Foundation ↔ Labs model where commercial success requires standard adoption.

What We Ask

Founding member status, working group access, Alliance introductions, implementation and diffusion funding, and a compliance feedback loop.

The Urgency

IEEE 7012 was approved November 4, 2025 and published January 20, 2026. AI agents deploying now. The window before surveillance architectures lock in: 2–3 years. agentprivacy is already building.

Defensible Headline Claim

IEEE 7012-2025 provides the agreement layer agentprivacy's dual-agent architecture requires. Compliance with the standard is a precondition for the Σ (agent) axis being measurable, and enables bilateral chronicles that can serve as evidentiary basis for VRCs. Standard compliance alone is not equivalent to the full agentprivacy architecture; the standard specifies agreement, not enforcement.
