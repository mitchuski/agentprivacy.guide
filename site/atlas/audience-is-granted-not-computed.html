💡 audience is granted, not computed

concept · shared · knowledge

The overlay's refusal of transitive trust: 'does trust reach' is a true question about a graph, but the map asks 'who may see', and answering the second with the first is how a trust score gets built by accident. Two hops is not half of link — it is nothing. Reachability is not visibility; no threshold required.
