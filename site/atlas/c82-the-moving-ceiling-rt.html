💡 C82 · The Moving Ceiling R(t)

concept · shared · knowledge

Adversary informational capability grows against fixed archives: the linkage corpus and side priors accumulate along calendar time, shrinking H(X|B_t) while nothing is added to the archive and no action of the subject is involved; R_inf(t) drifts upward on a schedule and every static reconstruction guarantee has a finite shelf life t*. Frontier-model releases enter only informationally (better extraction of linkage from existing corpora), never as compute against the information-theoretic guarantee, which is compute-saturated. Re-typed 2026-07-17 (L149); erosion form proven conditional in WP-07 Def 3.9 + Cor 5.4b; the conjectural content is the RATE. Active (65% · Run 1).
