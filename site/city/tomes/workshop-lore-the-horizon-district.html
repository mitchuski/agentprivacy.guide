The Horizon District

The cryptographic-durability district. The eastern watch. The path to PQC.

Vertex: V35 · 100011 · Stratum 3 (Protection + Computation + Value — defence · the quantum threat ·
the stake, in one address)
Witness register: Durability / migration-horizon
Opened: v1.8.0 · 2026-06-09 · Tome IX Act 1 The Measuring of the Dawn
Origin: seeded from the ecdsa.fail benchmark (shor_mage/); caused in-world by The Last Premine
surfacing through the Archivist's library.
Specs: 12 — The Validation Protocol. Skills:
role/agentprivacy-cryptographic-durability · meta/agentprivacy-horizon-gate.

Honest framing (binds the whole district): resource estimation is a durability signal, not an
attack. No claim that ECDSA is practically broken; no claim that any system is fully post-quantum safe.

The trust task — five expressive forms

  1. The Ceremony of the 9024 Witnesses (Dokimé). A petitioner brings a claimed cheaper circuit;
    Dokimé seats 9024 = 141 × 64 Fiat-Shamir witnesses drawn from a hash of the petitioner's own op-stream
    (so they cannot be chosen). The claim must clear all four validity gates (Spec 12). A claim that pleased
    a probe but fails the full set is named a nonce-island mirage and turned away.
  2. The Touchstone Assay (Dokimé). The claim is streaked across the lydite; true gold holds its colour
    across every witness, gilt does not. Operationalises "you cannot trust a claim you could gild."
  3. The Mosca Reckoning (Eos). From the resource estimate, report the distance to the dawn via Mosca's
    X + Y > Z — refusing both false comfort and false alarm. Durability as honest horizon-reporting.
  4. The RCI Liturgy / "Tony → Anton" (Eos). The bounded-change rite: state-the-waste → single-smallest-fix
    → confirm-or-reject-with-metrics. One bounded change at a time.
  5. The Migration Crossing (Poros). Inventory every dependent · cross each to a PQC successor · re-key —
    admitted only when every dependent has actually crossed (no silent stragglers).

Artefacts

  • The Horizon Glass (Eos) — a borne tool (like Pleione's astrolabe): shows the time-to-dawn; updates
    as the frontier moves.
  • The Touchstone (Dokimé) — a trinket, witness-capable: records that a claim faced its 9024 and held.
  • The Crossing-Ledger (Poros) — records a completed migration.

The dormant annex — the Salvage Yard

A dormant annex of the Navigation Quarter (Pleione 🧭, V44), activation-gated on the Horizon District
being built. A board where crews post post-quantum and reversible circuits as digital salvage — the
in-world home of the actual ecdsa.fail / trailmix work — settling through Dokimé's 9024 assay. No keeper
summoned yet; vertex assigned on activation. Origin: The Last Premine's quantum-salvage bounties. The
City's first instance of the dormant-annex structural pattern (status: dormant + activation_gate).
