## What this constellation is for

the Chart Shop occupies the *attentional register* — the fourth structural workshop register, distinct from *artefact-producing*, *civic-coordinating*, and *spawning*. It is the workshop for *holding*, *comparing*, *suspending*, and *mapping* qualitative material *before* it is forged into consensus, cloaked into artefact, or sealed into a Knowledge-Graph episode.

Existing City of Mages workshops sit on the *verb* axis: they all *do something to* material in. the Chart Shop sits on the *holding* axis: it preserves material *until* the bearer decides which other workshop to release it to (or whether to release it at all). A Chart House visit may produce no downstream artefact at all — the navigator may simply learn that her map does not yet fit, and leave with the map intact.

## The Hold · Compare · Map ceremony (working hypothesis)

The ceremony grammar is provisional and awaits the runecraft-protocol integration pass. The three-verb working hypothesis:

### Phase 1 · Hold

The navigator arrives at the dock with a constellation she carries — a working set of interpretations, uncertainties, models held across many minds. the Chart Shop admits the constellation into suspension. The Φ-gap (golden-ratio separation, conjecture C54) is the structural protection: nothing about the held constellation is forced into a schema, indexed for retrieval, or made legible to surveillance engines until the bearer chooses.

### Phase 2 · Compare

The navigator reads the held constellation against the City's existing tomes, against other constellations she has carried in prior visits, and against the constellations the workshop holds in suspension from other navigators (those that the bearers have opted to share at this register). The astrolabe is the instrument that performs the comparison — measuring altitudes, marking ratios, identifying constellations that share structure.

### Phase 3 · Map

If a constellation has matured — if its uncertainties have narrowed enough that mapping is honest — the bearer releases it. Three release destinations are admissible:

1. **To the Dragon Bonfire** — for forging into consensus reality (the constellation becomes a public claim, a knowledge-graph episode, a tome act)
2. **To the Weavers** — for cloaking into a Refractive Disclosure artefact (the constellation is published as cloaked-context rather than as plain claim)
3. **Back to the open sea** — for further wandering (the constellation was not yet ready; the bearer carries it on)

Releasing back to the open sea is *first-class*. the Chart Shop does not require an artefact-output. The discipline of holding-without-binding is itself the workshop's gift.
