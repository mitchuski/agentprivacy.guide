TileGlyph

Colored, animated navigation tiles carrying an emoji or ASCII glyph. The hand follows the Spellbook Ornaments style: two greys + white, thin lines, EB Garamond spaced caps, and a silver sparkle field whose polarity is meaning (+ = I will, − = I will not).

Scaffolded with create-wiki-plugin as wiki-plugin-tileglyph.

The DSL

Edit any tile (double-click) and use these lines:

Selene
glyph: 🌙
color: #3d7c47
category: Cosmological
anim: pulse
sparkle: 2+ 2-

The first bare line is the label and the page it links to. anim is one of pulse · float · spin · glow · none. sparkle sets the promise polarity of the field.
