# Proverbs Of The Trust Overlay

Nine, compressed from the [[Field Guide Trust Overlay]] work window, 15–16 August 2026. Written to the privacymage proverb form — *Template — Proverb*, on the spellbook site next door, not yet flagged into this neighbourhood: short, held in tension, promise-shaped, and true to a stranger who never read a word of the canon.

Each one is a thing the work refused to do, said in a breath. Where a proverb has a test that would fail if it were broken, the test is named — a proverb with a property under it is a promise with a witness.

**On publication** ⊥

> I will show you where I stood.
> I will not tell the world I stood anywhere.

*The ladder answers who. It was never asked how many.* — [[A Wiki Fork Is Not An Audience]]

**On distance** ⊥

> I will give you what we made together.
> I will not price the distance between us.

*The moment a step counts for something, everyone starts walking in circles.* — [[Audience Is Granted Not Computed]]

**On the gate** ⊥

> A gate that names what it will pass, holds.
> A gate that names what it will refuse, leaks.

*Everything unnamed walks through the second one, and the test still says green.*

**On the proof** ⊥

> I will not trust your proof because you handed it to me.
> I will trust it because I made it again.

*The proposer and the prover are held apart, or they are one hand.* — [[The Meet Rite]]

**On evidence** ⊥

> Prose will promise anything.
> Only a test will refuse.

*A privacy claim you cannot run is a mood.*

**On agreement** ⊥

> Two honest hands still part at the halfway point.
> Name the rule, or the gap is yours.

*One rounds up, one rounds to even, and the pin lands across the street.* — [[Rounding Is Not A Detail]]

**On the empty answer** ⊥

> An empty answer is still a true one.
> A borrowed answer is a debt.

*A fixture that outlives its stand-in becomes a lie the product tells.* — [[Phase 0 Acceptance]]

**On erosion** ⊥

> That I was there will not fade.
> That you were there, will.

*Your own trail is memory. A peer's old enthusiasm is only advice.* — [[Erosion Is A Rate Not A Cliff]]

**On the name** · triune

> I will not swear I am the only one of me.
> I will swear I am the same one to you.
> And a different one to everyone else.

*Refuse the claim you cannot keep. Give the one you can. Choose who receives it.* — [[The Pairwise Peer Reference]]
