Virtual Private Knowledge

This is the working site of the VPK pathway lane — a system for handing someone a slice of a private knowledge base over a tailnet, at a fidelity they have earned, and being able to say afterwards exactly what crossed.

It is written here because a federated wiki is what it governs. Every page is forkable, and a fork is how you disagree with it.

What this is not

Not a proof of understanding. Not a cryptographic guarantee. Not a claim about anyone else’s network. Each page says its own limits; see What This Does Not Prove.
