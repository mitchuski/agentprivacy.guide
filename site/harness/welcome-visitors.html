⿻ The Harness — the dual-agent core

A harness for two agents who are not allowed to collude. soulbae 🧙 proposes, soulbis ⚔️ proves, and between them sits the Gap ⿻ — held-out tests derived by hashing the proposal with a run secret the proposer never sees, so neither side chose them. Above them both is the First Person 😊, who alone opens the door.

And the whole system is built with trust-graph construction in mind: a gated result seals as a κ-addressed node, a signature mints an edge — a running harness accretes a verifiable trust graph as a by-product of doing its work (The Trust Graph Dialect).
