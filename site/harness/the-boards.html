🏁 The Boards

Every mage instance of the agentprivacy dual-agent harness that ran against a public competitive board, with its standing as each lane recorded it. Counts are honest: rejections, closures and unsubmitted work are listed beside the wins. Numbers stay in the lanes' ledgers; this page points to the public traces.

What the words mean

  • promoted — the board accepted the entry onto its leaderboard.
  • passed — an automated judge accepted the claim; promotion is a separate owner decision.
  • verified — the board's own server checked the result.
  • rejected — submitted and not taken (usually non-improving by the time it was judged).
  • unsubmitted — built and gated locally, deliberately held.

The lane binds note to root and root to bytes; it does not bind the judge to the verdict. Credit

Where an entry composes other solvers' work, they are credited as co-authors in the submission and this lane claims only its method layer. Where another solver built on a lane's ledger, the chain is stated, not contested.
