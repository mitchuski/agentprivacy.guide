C82

The Moving Ceiling: adversary informational capability grows against fixed archives: the linkage corpus and side priors accumulate along calendar time, shrinking H(X \ | B_t) while nothing is added to the archive and no action of the subject is involved; R_inf(t) drifts upward on a schedule and every static reconstruction guarantee has a finite shelf life t. Frontier-model releases enter only informationally (better extraction of linkage from existing corpora), never as compute against the information-theoretic guarantee, which is compute-saturated.
