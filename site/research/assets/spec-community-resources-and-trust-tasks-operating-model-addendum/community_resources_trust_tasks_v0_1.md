# Community Resources and Trust Tasks — Operating Model Addendum

Addendum version 0.1 · 9 September 2026 · Proposed operating model

**Version relationship:** companion to Whitepaper body v6.3 / V6 edition and PVM V6. No theorem, formal model version, token mechanism or governance rule is adopted by this addendum.

**Change record:** 0.1 introduces shared resource pools, six expressions of value, maintainer continuity, scoped pledges, registry distinctions and an equipped City Key journey. Future operating revisions increment this addendum separately from mathematical research versions.

Labs is developing a way to resource public goods across the agentprivacy ecosystem. This guide connects funding, contributions, workshops and carried journeys. It describes an operating proposal, not an adopted economic constitution or a deployed DAO.

## Resource the community

Four shared pools support compute, AI model tokens, energy/gas feeds and time. Shared capacity can serve several projects; attribute its cost once. Maintainers and the First Persons who hold project context have a named provision within Time. The current genesis shares and maintainer reserve are planning assumptions for review, not measured costs or spending authority.

The foundation has no fixed final funding total. Its resource ceiling is reviewed against the next operating period, capacity already available and changing community needs. This operational resource ceiling is distinct from the model's informational reconstruction ceiling R(t). The first funding milestone is a checkpoint. Numerical allocations and detailed project illustrations are maintained in [Labs funding](https://agentprivacy.org/fund/) and its [versioned funding data](https://agentprivacy.org/fund/funding-plan.json); do not duplicate those mutable numbers throughout the corpus.

## Six expressions of value

- **Compute:** machines, inference, useful runs and reproducible capacity.
- **Memory:** maintained context, private knowledge and source continuity.
- **Connection:** consented relationships, introductions and collaboration.
- **Delegation:** bounded work, open models, skills and accountable agent mandates.
- **Protection:** privacy engineering, security review and independent verification.
- **Value:** exchange, contributions and accountable stewardship.

All six express value. The Value bit concerns exchange and stewardship specifically. Private knowledge chiefly touches Memory and Connection; Protection governs access and Delegation limits permitted uses. Contributing a consultation or reference does not transfer ownership or imply permission for model training. Funding need profiles use a separate versioned schema, not a mutation of identity or City Key posture. The current runtime bit order is Protection, Delegation, Memory, Connection, Compute, Value; 010000 means Delegation. Earlier differently ordered references remain historical and need explicit convention labels.

## A pledge is a trust task

A pledge states who promises what, to whom, with purpose, resource limit, access terms, evidence and acceptance criteria. [TrustTask](https://trusttask.org/) is the requested conceptual reference; compatibility with a specific external protocol must be checked, not assumed. A delivery claim remains distinct from an accepted result. Revision, withdrawal, partial delivery and dispute require their own records.

The Labs board currently makes local, unregistered, unsigned drafts. It has no payment processing, admission backend or shared registry. Its membership and workshop-starting proposals require agreed terms and actual scoped admission. Payment alone grants no vote, identity, ownership, workshop position or treasury permission.

Three functions remain distinct: a **content registry** resolves an artefact; a **pledge registry** retains commitments and review history; **relationship/identity verification** checks issuer, holder, scope, status and evidence. A content hash or pledge UUID is not a verified VRC. A registry operator does not decide spending authority. Private evidence remains under agreed access, and even public metadata requires deliberate disclosure.

## Equip, encounter and carry onward

Use [Begin](https://agentprivacy.org/begin/) for a bounded ecosystem survey, a harness/persona/skill equipment card and a purpose-led journey through the corpus. Follow [the route map](https://agentprivacy.org/begin/routes.json), then ask what resonated and offer a primary next step and a neighbouring alternative. Do not infer comprehension or consent from browsing.

Use [Mages City's entry kit](https://mages.city/skill.md) and [MCP routes](https://mages.city/mcp-entry.md). Inspect the actual available tools before calling them. With no MCP, retain a document-based journey ledger. Preserve original evidence privately and use supported key/journey operations only within the existing mandate. A walk is not a credential, and a selected skill is not demonstrated competence. Keep reading, practice, reviewed outcomes and current permissions distinguishable.

The [private knowledge network](https://guide.agentprivacy.ai/guide/the-private-knowledge-network.html) describes invitation-based shared wiki space and small-machine embassies, including Raspberry Pi hosts. It supports knowledge continuity across workshops; HH Workshop is one related teaching and consent-led workflow, not the owner of all shared infrastructure. Actual network access and policy must be checked at the service.

## A constitution formed in practice

Mages establish workshops, run scoped agents and form verifiable trust agents and communities into a network at the speed of trust. Proposals, independent review, governed authorisation, bounded execution and receipts form a learning loop. Record promised, approved, released and used resources separately, with shared identifiers, allocation revision and accounting period.

Privacy Is Value informs questions of consent, context and who benefits. It does not calculate a fair project budget or prove an economic return. The constitution develops with participants: allocation rules, maintainer appointments, membership terms, dispute processes, treasury mandates and registry policies remain to agree. Privacy pools, stablecoins and Net Dollar are candidate settlement approaches, not connected capabilities claimed here.

This operating proposal complements the existing V6 research; it does not amend its equations or proofs. The VRC economic architecture is one implementation option. Earlier fixed ceremony-price assumptions and fee splits are historical design inputs, not current Labs membership policy.
