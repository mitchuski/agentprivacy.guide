Welcome Visitors

This site is a reflection. It argues one thing: an AI agent working against a persistent, governed wiki is the natural shape of a self-improving research loop - and that the protocols I have been building, the Hitchhiker rewilding, and Andrej Karpathy's LLM-Wiki idea are all the same logic in different skins.

For David: this is a draft I am leaving on the homepage so you can absorb the understanding and reflection, then fork it into the right subdomain. Read the four pages below in order; the whole argument is self-contained and interlinked, ready to lift across.

The one-line claim

RAG re-derives knowledge on every query and throws the answer away. An LLM-Wiki compiles knowledge once, keeps it current, and files good answers back as new sources - so the work compounds instead of resetting. That single move - query output becoming wiki input - is the self-improving loop. We did not theorise it; we ran it on two real corpora.

The trust layer

The loop above compounds knowledge. This thread compounds trust - the bilateral agreements a first person proposes (IEEE 7012 / MyTerms), and the agents, devices, and trust communities that carry and witness them.

  • myTerms: The Agreement Layer - one agreement in three registers (human / legal / machine-readable); why Godel makes the perfect agreement impossible, so trust needs a witness from outside; and the consent-first LAN ceremony - around a shared model that names itself - that earns one.

This is filed as a working example of the loop it describes.
