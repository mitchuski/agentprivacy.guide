Internal strategy session for the Hitchhiker / Game of 42 project — planning the gentle, teaser-style July 4th launch of Robbie's declaration and the brand.

An internal strategy conversation for the Hitchhiker / Game of 42 project, working through the imminent release — in two or three weeks, around July 4th — of Robbie's "declaration" and the brand identity, messaging and teaser campaign that wrap it. The team interrogates the launch with four framing questions: who cares, who is involved, what has changed, and what proof shows it is working. A central tension is pacing: rather than a splashy, in-your-face launch, they agree on a deliberately gentle, subtle, tonal rollout — a stealth/teaser manifesto distributed to the 42 "vision fish," with the bolder, splashier moment reserved for Burning Man in September and the Not Panicking Festival beyond. They map the audiences (the H2G2 / hardcore Hitchhiker community, the "no-gens" who may care most, academics, AI frontier labs, artists, coders, the OpenServe and web3 communities) and the people each member would personally invite, while flagging risks like Hitchhiker-community pushback over who speaks for them, impersonators, and rogue meme coins (with "Bushido" IP-defence jiu-jitsu as the response). Success is measured less by mass social reach and more by an increase in serendipity: a surge of one-on-one Zoom calls and meetings with potential vision fish, conferences and venues.

Key points

  • Framing questions: the launch is examined through four lenses, each answered round-robin by the team — who cares, who is involved, what has changed, and what is the proof/evidence it's working.
  • Tonal, not splashy: consensus is a gentle, subtle, teaser-style rollout (a new X/Twitter account posting the manifesto, no paid advertising); the earliest justified splashy bold launch is Burning Man in September.
  • Who cares (July 4th audience): Hitchhiker / H2G2 hardcore community, the no-gens (who may care more than AI-proliferation believers), universities, AI frontier labs, artists, coders, the OpenServe community, web3/AI, plus BBC Radio 4 pickup; the date ties to the US Constitution's 250th anniversary.
  • Who is involved: the mice, Henry, and the prepared vision fish (front and centre, though 42 won't be reached by launch); the personal intro networks — Mitch's ~30-40 contacts in the City of Mages and agent-privacy Telegram chats, the lady who wrote the prophecy book, and Berlin contacts (the Swarm, Bonfires).
  • Robbie's invite list: figures who may take months to say yes — Larry Lessig / the Creative Commons + constitutional lawyer, the Swarm or Ethereum Foundation, a stand-up comedian, a theatre-arts group, and a push to invite more women to the mice.
  • Underground / viral channels: underground media, an NDA-gated first-look deal tied to the OpenServe campaign, a hardcore Turkish H2G2 spin-off (our man's group), and Alina's autistic-network and octopus/squid network as viral seeds.
  • Risks & defence: fear of memes landing in explosive situations before readiness; impersonators and fake Telegram accounts; rogue meme coins (pump.fun already hosts Marvin/Zaphod coins); the response is Bushido — defensive patenting / IP-defence of the commons with humour and martial-arts jiu-jitsu.
  • What has changed: network expanded beyond the core team, first social-media presence, press releases out, 42 events and podcast guests being booked, public association with Robbie and the Hitchhiker community, and the project being taken seriously with potential for virality in algorithms they aren't even aware of.
  • Proof it's working: retweets/redistribution/echo in the weeks after, increased confidence going into conversations, inbound tech-journalist contact, and chiefly an increase in serendipity — a jump in one-on-one Zoom calls and meetings with potential vision fish, conferences and venues (e.g. RSA, Don't Panic festival).

Action items

  • Finalise the declaration plus brand identity, messaging and teaser-campaign materials for release in ~2-3 weeks, targeting July 4th.
  • Stand up a new X / Twitter account to post the manifesto in a deliberately gentle, subtle tone; no paid advertising at this stage.
  • Robbie to send the teaser manifesto to the prepared vision fish (toward 42) and draft his personal invite list (e.g. Larry Lessig / Creative Commons lawyer, Swarm/Ethereum Foundation, comedian, theatre-arts group).
  • Mitch to reach out to his ~30-40 City of Mages / agent-privacy Telegram contacts and to the prophecy-book author, announcing the Hitchhiker x privacy-research connection.
  • Max to give a lead to the Berlin contacts (the Swarm, Bonfires) similar to the OpenServe relationship.
  • Pursue underground/viral channels: NDA-gated first-look with OpenServe campaign, the Turkish H2G2 spin-off, and Alina's autistic + octopus/squid networks.
  • Inform the core weekly H2G2 community ahead of launch (interviews, podcast, branding) so the rollout isn't a shock.
  • Define a Bushido protocol for IP-defence of the commons — defensive patenting, the right lawyers, calling out trivialisation/fraud (rogue meme coins, impersonators) with humour.
  • Track success by counting meetings/Zoom calls booked with potential vision fish, conferences, venues and mice — measure the increase in serendipity rather than mass social reach.
