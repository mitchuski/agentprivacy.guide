How it 'becomes real'

Asked directly. Asked "what would make this feel like it's a success of making it real to us?" the honest first answer is small and concrete: "I think playing the game, that would be really fun... that's pretty clear." The deeper realness is relational and lore-bound: interviewing "the original developers of the H2G2 game," to "get that meme out there," "and then just get an official partnership." The felt logic is named plainly: "connecting the old school law [lore] to the, what is happening now would make it feel real," because "they're just heroes of the indie gaming community," lifting it above "just a flash in the pan indie game."

Texture from elsewhere. Realness also lands as a structural milestone, not just feeling: "It also feels like a kind of milestone for establishing the IP remixable part," where "you can use these characters and these terms, and if you fork from one person's game to another game or reuse the code, this is how it works." The endorsement fantasy carries warmth: "hey, yeah, let's remake that kind of thing."
