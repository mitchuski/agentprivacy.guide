# The Hitchhiker's Timeline
### The core briefing: the Game of 42, from a quiet declaration to a planet that thinks together without a centre

*The canonical map for the Vision Fish, held by the mice within the Game of 42, a constructive instance of the Privacy Is Value model.*

---

## How to read this

This is the single map that holds the **Hitchhiker's Timeline** (the **Game of 42**) together: the run of declarations, festivals, infrastructure, guides and moonshots from mid 2026 to the **Annual Galactic Meeting on 4 July 2027**, and on into the 2028 horizon. It was drawn from a sequence of working sessions in which the mice (chiefly Robbie, Max, Dave and Mitch, with Henry on brand) ran every milestone through the same five questions. Each milestone keeps its own wiki with the full session behind it; this document is the map that stops them wandering off.

If you are reading this as a prospective **Vision Fish**, someone we are inviting to pledge time, lend judgement, and lend a name, start here and read to the end, where your place is set out plainly.

One standing note. These were visioning sessions, so the dates are intentions and not commitments, and the headline figures (forty-two thousand users, $4.2M, forty-two billion tokens) are motifs and targets, not forecasts. We plan past what is built and then back-cast. Where something is proven we say so; where it is only hoped for, we mark it as such.

## 1. The wager

Almost every story about the future bends toward a single point: one model, one centre, one moment in which everything is decided for everyone, and beneath it one enormous lake into which the data of every person is poured and mined. We are betting the other way. Not one singularity but many; not a centre but a federation of local sovereignties; not extraction but its opposite.

The destination has a name: the **poly-singularity**. It is the point at which a plurality of local, self-governing communities, each holding its own boundary, its own model, its own assembly, its own answer, federates into one global mutual without any of them surrendering what makes it local. The inversion is economic as much as technical: value is realised not by pooling data but by keeping the boundary. Privacy stops being a cost to manage and becomes the thing that produces worth. This is the proposition Mitch has formalised as the Privacy Is Value model, now in its sixth version, the moving-ceiling R(t) line of work, with a small set of proven bounds and a larger set of clearly-labelled conjectures. Many singularities, one commons, no centre. The whole timeline is the path that earns it.

## 2. The world, in brief

The project speaks in *Hitchhiker's Guide* idiom throughout, and the vocabulary is load-bearing: it is the operating system of the culture as much as the technology.

**The people.** *Mice*, the core builders. *Vision Fish*, respected advisors and early backers who pledge 42 hours and lend their network (the target is 42 of them; you are one). *Patron Fish*, funders who donate or invest 42k. *Hitchhikers*, the general membership. *Guides*, both the people who author a guide and the unit of work itself; capital-G the Guide is the overarching brand.

**The agents.** *Marvin*, the flagship model, in two forms: the foundational model and the *Marvelette*, a baby Marvin that runs locally on a Raspberry Pi. *Eddie*, the local shipboard computer. *Deep Thought*, the cloud, the frontier model. The pairing Eddie and Deep Thought is how the team narrates the local-versus-centralised tension honestly, from both sides.

**The pair beneath the brand.** Two figures hold the architecture: the **Swordsman** (Soulbis), who sets the boundary, and the **Mage** (Soulbae), who casts the connection. The sword alone is a wall; the mage alone is a flood. Held together they are a river: water given banks, a boundary that lets something flow rather than drown. The brand mark is a Vision Fish swimming through the place where the two squares cross, the boundary and the connection overlapping.

**The motif, 42.** Everywhere, on purpose: 42 hours pledged, 42k contributions, 42 Vision Fish, 42 conferences, 42 locations, groups of 42, 42 billion tokens of donated compute. Items that do not fit the calendar are placed "on the 42", an off-calendar holding space.

**The heart, the Trust Graph.** A knowledge graph (a guide's content) overlaid on a promise graph (staked time and money). The relationship between the two is the trust graph, drawn.

## 3. The method

Four ideas govern how the whole plan is built, and a Vision Fish will meet them again and again.

The **five framing questions**: every milestone runs the same sequence (who cares; who is involved; what changes; what is the proof; what makes it feel real), and only those that can answer the fifth survive. **Managed events and announcements**: managed events (a festival, a launch) are fixed points you back-cast from; announcements future-cast forward, pulling energy toward a thing by saying it will happen. **Festivals are punctuation**: they are not the thing but where an ongoing series of Hitchhiker Hackathons surfaces in public. And **the speed of trust**: the team can control its own work but can only express intent toward money and partnerships, so it builds in a deliberate free month for serendipity to land, and grows slowly, holding integrity, on the principle that a movement which outruns its own trust is not a movement but an incident.

The two-tier target: by the AGM (4 July 2027), 42,000 users and $4.2M to offer as prizes; by the next cycle (2028), 4.2M members and $42M.

The plan has three kinds of moving part: the *events* we hold (the milestones along the river), the *convergences* we steer toward (the AGM, and the poly-singularity beyond it), and the *focuses* that occupy the mice between events. A walk through the calendar follows. This year is 2026; the great convergence is the AGM on 4 July 2027; the horizon reaches into 2028.

**Declaration and Brand Launch (4 July 2026).** The first line in the sand: Robbie's Declaration and the brand, released in a multi-perspectival, poetic-translation form. The defining decision is tone, a quiet teaser with no paid advertising, sent toward the 42; the louder messaging waits for Burning Man in September. The people who care first are the strangest, the *no-gens* who reject generative AI most of all. The proof wanted is serendipity, not reach. A defensive posture, *Bushido*, protects the commons with the right lawyers and humour.

**Building the ship (August 2026).** The substrate, in two halves: the Pan-Galactic Time Bank and its accounting (the plumbing), and Hitchhiker TV with the Trust Graph visualisation (the public face), the first time the Declaration's words become something you can see, changing live from real data.

**The Don't Panic Tour (September to December 2026).** The first guides, the Marvin PR tour, a vibe-coding call-out, and a 24/7 radio whose claim to realness is that it is on. Student energy refreshes a base grown top-heavy; diversity of age and belief is mechanical, not decorative.

**Web Summit (October 2026).** The project steps onto a real stage; the guide apps ship; the first Patron Fish sign on at 42k; and Hitchhikers get on-chain financial privacy through Privacy Pools, pitched as a positive-use case rather than a compliance tool.

**The Don't Panic Festival (5 November 2026).** A decentralised event across 42 locations, attended by cooking at home, turning on the radio, and refusing to panic. The tone is the product, and the reframe is that "Don't Panic" is a governance meme, not an event meme. No project meme coin, firmly.

**Winter and the Game Jam (November 2026 to January 2027).** The fair token launch sits in this window; winter hackathons keep the builders warm; and a January game jam, ideally co-run with the Global Game Jam, treats other game-makers as an audience rather than competitors and settles the remixable-IP rules.

**Groundhog Day (2 February 2027).** The script flips from gamers to institutions, timed as the project's own un-Davos. Patron commitments harden into money; a discipline holds underneath, that bad money is bad money however much of it there is.

**Spring, Myspace Rewilded (Spring 2027).** The aesthetic and cultural turn, and the moment the philosophy becomes touchable: everyone vibe-codes their own interface onto one shared substrate, many expressions of a single thing instead of one corporate feed.

**The Annual Galactic Meeting (4 July 2027).** The great convergence, across 42 venues: part awards night, part future-casting, part party, and the formal end of the founding process as the IP passes into mutual ownership. The Improbable Lottery, the Burn Night where fluid contribution becomes solid, a Pledge Day, and the launch of the Dolphin Fund.

## 5. The 2028 horizon

Past the AGM, the mice back-cast their deepest intent. This is the least-developed and most aspirational part of the plan, and the reason the project exists. A second AGM, around 4 July 2028, takes aim at 4.2M members and $42M.

**Living Sovereign Compute (5 November 2027).** A viable, self-sustaining, peer-to-peer network of sovereign data centres and home labs, with a credible chance of out-computing the great foundation models on their own ground. Wanted by the data-dignity movement, indigenous communities, universities, libraries and governments that are neither American nor Chinese. The honest conflict, that you cannot easily launch an alternative to the frontier labs while courting them as partners, is resolved by sequence and ceremony: provoke first, then route the labs through the citizen assemblies and a trust ceremony before they reach the commons.

**The poly-singularity (2028).** Two faces of one arrival. The governance face (the Unsingularity, around February 2028): a planetary structure of assemblies in which nature, the young, the indigenous and the unrecognised all hold a seat, deliberation rather than survey. The answers face (Deep Thought Realized, around June 2028): the frontier labs bequeath billions of tokens and a pool of compute to a neutral third space, so credentialed Hitchhikers can work the great questions in the open, with a non-monetary path in through pledged hours. Together: every community holds its own boundary and its own model, and the plurality federates into one mutual that can answer the planet's largest questions without anyone surrendering their ground. Many singularities, and no centre.

## 6. The systems beneath

The calendar is the surface. Five interlocking systems run underneath; almost every milestone is an expression of one.

The **Trust Graph** (knowledge overlaid with promise; a constellation that hardens, as trust solidifies, into a city with squares, the City of Mages). The **Pan-Galactic Time Bank** (pledge 42 hours, receive 42 credits, summing to zero, with a portion donated or invested into guides; threshold contracts auto-refund if a target is not met; hours are an honesty box, a top lawyer and an eight-year-old billing the same rate). The **Game of 42** (start solo, find complements, reach six, then bud: seven groups of six make 42, and to grow further you bud another rather than bloat). The **three bonding curves** (money, hours, trust; a guide private on one axis must be public on another, so it cannot be attacked on both at once). And **guides, agents, media and money** (gu.id.earth, the agents, Hitchhiker Radio and TV, a crypto-economic paper, patron.fish, a fair token launch, and the Dolphin Fund).

## 7. What "becoming real" means

Across the sessions the same answers recur, and together they are this team's working definition of success, which is not the usual one. Serendipity over reach (inbound meetings, not follower counts). People pitching it back to you. Emotion (the test is that people should be moved enough to cry, and unembarrassed enough to mention it at a family dinner). The map filling in (the globe freckled with pins; 42 venues organising; nodes coming online; stars and forks). Money that does not feel extractive. And something you can point to: the visualisation, shimmering live from real data.

## 8. Open questions

The discipline is to raise the hard questions early and pin them where they can be seen. These are the threads where a Vision Fish's judgement is worth most: the 5 November collision (a conference stage and a 42-location festival on the same night); the IP transfer timing in 2027 (the tokens and Marvin before the IP enters the new company); the frontier-labs relationship (partnership resolved through the assembly trust ceremony, acquisition terms open); governance when it succeeds (who allocates donated compute, and is it locked down); the environmental critique of compute, named the chief danger and as yet unanswered; lottery legality across jurisdictions; the standing vigilance against bad money; and the trust-graph visualisation itself, left open on purpose, as an invitation.

## 9. Where you fit

A Vision Fish is not a spectator. The early milestones are where your judgement and your network matter most, an introduction, a stage, a willingness to lend your name while lending it still costs something; the later ones are where that early trust compounds into governance and compute at planetary scale. The wager is the one in Section 1: that a federation of kept boundaries, each holding its own model and its own voice, can become a single global mutual in which privacy is realised as value. We have a word for the condition we are aiming at, kept deliberately lowercase: eutierria, the state of belonging to a living system that has kept your boundary intact.

We are not asking you to admire the plan. We are asking you to test it, and to help us walk it. There is a seat, one of forty-two, with your name on it.
