# Proverbs of the Game of 42
### a workshopping collection drawn from the whole: a quarry, not a monument

*Forty-two proverbs and a handful of spells, distilled from the timeline so the architecture can be carried in the mouth. Offered in the spirit of the Relationship Proverb Protocol: a proverb is a promise compressed until it can be remembered. Carry them the way you carry a towel, lightly and everywhere. These are seed-lines. Cut them, invert them, argue with them. Where a line carries a [variant], both are live and neither has won yet.*

---

## i. the wager

**1.** A lake with no shore is only a fine place to drown. Give it banks and it remembers it was a river, and a river will take you somewhere.

**2.** They told us privacy was the fare. It turned out to be the ride.

**3.** A centre is one convenient address for all the bad news to arrive at once.

**4.** Many singularities, one commons, and no throne worth the fight. Mostly harmless, at last.

**5.** What you pool, you lose. What you bound, you keep.
[variant: pool and you are mined; bound and you are paid.]

**6.** Do not ask who owns the water. Ask who was good enough to give it banks.

## ii. the boundary and the bond

**7.** The sword without the mage is a wall. The mage without the sword is a flood. Hold both, or hold nothing.

**8.** A gate that will not close is not a kindness. It is only an opening someone else found first.

**9.** Protect points inward, connect points out, and a self needs both directions at once.

**10.** Refusal is the first courtesy a boundary offers.

**11.** Two agents, one self: one to guard the threshold, one to stand outside it with a thumb out.

## iii. the trust graph

**12.** Trust is not declared. It is drawn.

**13.** Lay what you know over what you kept, and the shape between the two is your name.

**14.** Intent, then promise, then trust, and never skip the middle, for the middle is where the lying would live.

**15.** Keep your promises small and your graph will grow large.

**16.** A city is only a trust graph that held still long enough to be walked through.

## iv. the time bank and the giving

**17.** The hour of the lawyer and the hour of the child weigh the same. Weigh them so on purpose.

**18.** A ledger that sums to zero has nothing to hoard and nothing to hide.

**19.** Pledge the hours you have, not the hours you meant to have.

**20.** If the room does not fill, the contract hands your hours back. This is what honesty looks like once someone has automated it.

**21.** Give until it stops feeling like loss. That number is nearer than you fear.

## v. the game of 42

**22.** You cannot win by growing. You can only bud.

**23.** Six is a beginning, forty-two is a board, and the constellation is everyone else.

**24.** Forty-two is the answer they already handed us. The whole game is remembering the question.

**25.** A circle that only ever agrees with itself has quietly gone blind. Seat the stranger.

**26.** Diversity is not the ornament on the assembly. It is the load it was built to bear.

**27.** Open a game you can keep playing, not one you can win.

## vi. the road

**28.** Ask five things of every plan, and believe only the fifth: what makes it feel real.

**29.** Over-generate, then grieve, then prune. The pile is not waste. The pile is compost.

**30.** Grow at the speed of trust. Anything faster is only an incident wearing a crowd.

**31.** Pack light, leave one month empty for serendipity to land in, and never set out without a towel and a reason.

**32.** The improbable is only the probable you have not yet been introduced to.

## vii. the agents and the fish

**33.** Build for the small thing on the desk and the vast thing in the sky, and refuse to choose. The refusal is the architecture.

**34.** Let the machine sulk locally. Even an agent deserves a room with the door shut.

**35.** The fish swims through where the two squares overlap. The squares are the boundary, the overlap is the commons, and the swimming is the trust.

**36.** The eye of the vision fish stays open underwater. That is the entire trick.

**37.** A guide is three curves: what you gave, what you spent, who vouched. Be hidden on one and you must be shown on another.

## viii. the poly-singularity, and the close

**38.** Give the river a seat and it will vote through its watershed.

**39.** Deliberation is not a survey with better manners. Sit for a year, and then speak.

**40.** Many minds, each holding its own banks, do not flood together. They run as one river and still arrive as themselves.

**41.** If no one wept, you built a tool. If someone wept, you built a home.

**42.** Print *don't panic* on the cover, and then do the harder thing, which is to mean it.

## spells (rough, for the table)

These are longer than proverbs and not yet load-bearing. Read them aloud, which is the only real test.

**The Emergence Spell** *(after the one already carved at the door)*
> The right people arrive.
> The right moment happens.
> The right ending closes.
> You do not recruit a game. You open one,
> and trust the pattern, for the pattern trusts you.

**The Boundary Blessing**
> May your wall have a gate.
> May your gate have a keeper.
> May your keeper know your face.
> And may the water, when it comes asking,
> be given banks and a heading,
> and so become a river,
> and so become a road.

**The Traveller's Charge**
> Carry a towel.
> Carry a question.
> Stick out a thumb at the improbable.
> When the lift arrives, and it will,
> climb in,
> mind the boundary,
> tend the gap,
> discover the destination,
> don't panic.

**Coda: eutierria**
> When the work is done and the boundary still holds,
> there is a word for the quiet that follows:
> not victory, which needs a loser,
> but eutierria, the peace of belonging
> to a living thing that kept you whole.
>
> Don't panic. You are early, and you are welcome.
> So long, and thanks for all the boundaries.

---

*Workshop notes: proverbs are numbered for reference, not ranked. The eight headings are a re-decomposition rather than the briefing's five named systems (wager, boundary, trust, gift, game, road, agents, plurality is a finer cut), so do not expect a one-to-one match with the five-system canon; each proverb still traces back to the part of the architecture it compresses. Suggested moves for the next pass: tighten any line that needs more than one breath; find the three that are secretly the same proverb and keep the best; and mark which of these is true enough to carve and which is only clever.*
