Building Ship scoping session — who cares about and who is involved in the Pan Galactic Time Bank, Energy Accounting and Trust Graph visualization for the September launch.

A "who cares / who is involved" scoping session for the August work package the team dubs "Building Ship" — the infrastructure that must be in place ahead of the September launch of the Hitchhiker / Game of 42 project. The work splits into two parts: the Pan Galactic Time Bank + Energy Accounting system (for tracking admin effort and dynamic equity), and Hitchhiker TV + the Trust Graph visualization (how Zoom meetings are recorded and how the trust graph is rendered as something visual rather than "too wordy"). The group reasons that the time-bank/energy-accounting piece is "plumbing" — required, kept under the hood, of interest mainly to time-bankers, mutual-credit and slicing-the-pie people, and future accountants/lawyers — while the visualization piece is the public face that turns the Declaration's words into something people can see and experience. They map the participants (the mice, the Vision Fish, early 42-hour committers, OGM, design students, open-source library communities) and identify what changes once the tools exist: faster self-driven work, real data from real activity, more open doors, and a possible snowball moment. The session closes circling toward the September local planning, with a shared wish to watch the tetrahedron / trust-graph "shimmer and evolve" live from real data input.

Key points

  • Building Ship (August): the work package supporting the September launch, split into two parts — Pan Galactic Energy Accounting (Time Bank + dynamic equity) and Hitchhiker TV + Trust Graph visualization.
  • Time Bank = plumbing: required internal infrastructure to prove the team can cope with volunteers when they turn up; deliberately kept under the hood rather than led with, since time banking risks reading as a hippie project and turns off crypto / token-go-up people (who paradoxically care because it does NOT sound crypto).
  • Who cares about the Time Bank: the team itself (admin + dynamic equity), the mice, future accountants/lawyers, economic-white-paper readers, the time banks themselves, and the mutual-credit / slicing-the-pie crowd.
  • Trust Graph visualization is the public face: for the first time people exposed to the Declaration can see something that is not words; answers "what is a trust graph? oh, this is what it looks like" and serves media/event/conference people, decentralized-web/crypto media folk who lack original content, and the Don't Panic (Not Panicking) Festival narrative.
  • It is an open design challenge: representing the trust graph in a way which isn't just dots on the screen but really meaningful — a hook to invite information-theory students/professors, designers and OGM to live-code and help solve it.
  • Who is involved (build it ourselves): call-outs to time-bank and software-hackathon people to vibe code it with us; interviews with key time-banking and dynamic-equity figures; references to Holochain, Mary & Art (built the time bank pre-Holochain), Circles, UBI, and the Hitchhiker economy white paper.
  • Visualization people: Mitch (already doing weird visualizations), paid branding/graphic-design staff, Amp, Drew, Rachel and Jade (penciled in), Nicole's TV/design students (course starts September), Nordcraft (Figma-like tool emitting real CSS), the Open Global Mind (OGM) community, Obsidian, and the VDO.Ninja maintainer for vibe-coding Hitchhiker TV.
  • What changes: "I can bloody do my work" — Hitchhiker TV records/transcribes/uploads automatically, paperwork collapses, teams of 4-6 stay motivated via a slice of equity; a possible snowball/catalyst moment, real data arrives, and doors open because there's a real thing to point to.
  • Evidence & becoming real: data straight from the accounting/time-bank systems, productivity metrics, OGM excitement, press/demo requests, hitchhikers signing up, and the shared mental model clicking; the magic moment is the tetrahedron visualization changing live from real data (the Berlin group's star-on-a-screen, parameters changeable in an art room, but at larger scale).

Action items

  • Build the Pan Galactic Time Bank + Energy Accounting core in August; test the mechanism internally to gain confidence it can cope with volunteers.
  • Keep the time-bank/energy-accounting piece under the hood in messaging; do not lead public PR with time banking.
  • Issue a call-out to time-bank, software and hackathon people to come vibe code it with us; iterate.
  • Interview and consult key time-banking and dynamic-equity figures (and ask them to reach out to their communities); reference Holochain, Mary & Art, Circles, UBI.
  • Engage the initial 42-hour committers — the mice, the Vision Fish, the early slicing-the-pie/equity people — and experimental-value crypto contacts such as Myco.
  • Follow up with Amp, Drew, Rachel and Jade (penciled in for the visualization / ranch content stack).
  • Involve Mitch and the paid branding/UX team; thread their work into Hitchhiker TV, the website, videos, YouTube, events and conferences.
  • Involve Nicole's TV design students (course from September) and iterate the vibe-code design with them.
  • Reach out to design-platform/open-source communities — Nordcraft, OGM, Obsidian, and the VDO.Ninja library/community — to co-build the visualization and Hitchhiker TV.
  • Incentivize and pay branding/interface/UX people with the promise of a truly global stage (paid in time, money and fame — the AGM component).
  • Instrument the tools to capture real data from live activity; add design changes (more color change / motion visibility) so the visualization feels alive.
  • Carry the work into the September local planning session, aiming for a live tetrahedron/trust-graph that shimmers and evolves week by week from real data.
