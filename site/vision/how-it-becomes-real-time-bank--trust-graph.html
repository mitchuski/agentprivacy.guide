How it 'becomes real'

Asked directly. The question lands awkwardly: "a bit of a weird one" and "this one doesn't really fit." One honest answer is small and personal: realness is "hitchhikers like first persons signing up," and the moment "they will see an experience as a shared mental model" of trust graphs people currently "don't really get." The strongest felt-realness is private: "selfishly it's like I've got the tetrahedron but to actually see it change with an actual real data input would be a, this is becoming real, that is magic."

Texture from elsewhere. The wish is for it to happen "automatically" and to "shimmer evolve visually week by week." A sober correction tempers the magic: the change "will be minute at first and then it will become significant," needing months of data, though design tweaks could "increase the color changes or the visibility of the motion so that people feel that it's more alive." The image of growing "your tamagotchi" carries the tenderness.
