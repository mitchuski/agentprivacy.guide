Strategy session on absorbing volunteers at the speed of trust — the Pan Galactic Time Bank plus the Game of 42.

Summary

A strategy conversation tackling the central problem of how Hitchhiker / Game of 42 will absorb and organise volunteers — Robbie's "big fear" that the project succeeds wildly at getting its story out on social media and then drowns under more people turning up than it can satisfy. The proposed answer is to "grow at the speed of trust" by first recruiting and organising an army of skilled volunteers (the makers, trusted community leaders, vision fish and mice) who can later greet and facilitate the wider public, with AI augmenting the second phase. Two pillars carry the design: a classic-but-augmented "Pan Galactic Time Bank" (an offers-and-wants matching system with onboarding interviews, a skills ontology/graph, and a novel pledging mechanism that lets people pledge 42 hours and invest/donate credits into projects called guides), and the "Game of 42", a viral, time-bank-native pattern for budding small complementary groups (6 to 42, head/heart/hands plus three mirror roles, geometrically a flower/hexagon mapped onto the Heart of Gold spaceship and a deck of cards). Trust is treated as a self-regulating honesty-box / dating-game currency that sums to zero, backed by mobile, semi-audited cross-verified trust graphs rather than heavy security. The participants connect this to a three-bonding-curve identity for each guide (money / hours / trust-knowledge) and several playful surfaces — a tabletop/Reactable-style card game, a Civilization-style map, an annual Galactic Meeting with 'no kings, we're all kings' sanctification. The overriding decision: this only matters if the team vibe-codes it quickly and uses it internally first.

Key points

  • The core problem: handle a flood of volunteers without being overwhelmed; solution is a two-phase go-to-market — recruit/organise skilled volunteers first, then greet the general public (AI-assisted) second, growing at the speed of trust.
  • Pan Galactic Time Bank: a classic time bank (offers/wants database, hour-long onboarding interview seen as 'the spellbook', paid or volunteer broker) modernised with AI recording and matching against existing skills ontologies / a skills graph, surfacing gaps to send volunteers on quests to find missing skills (e.g. radio people).
  • Skill multiplication: target highly skilled people first (one privacy-architecture expert serves 42, each of those serves 42) so a few high-powered vision fish/mice virally seed large skilled cohorts; less-skilled people volunteer to greet/onboard/match.
  • Pledging mechanism (novel): pledge 42 hours then get 42 credits in a personal mutual-credit account (sums to zero, must be repaid), with a portion donatable/investable into guides that hold group bank accounts; pledge limits tied to reputation; smart-contract refund if a guide misses its pledge target (e.g. 4200 hours). No existing time bank does project/group pledging.
  • Currency philosophy: hours are a dating game, a 'Christian/communist currency' (top lawyer = eight-year-old child rate), an honesty box; collusion nets to zero so it doesn't matter financially but is socially policed and can use a verifier of time/outcome; built on a mobile, cross-verified personal trust-graph ledger (envisaged with bitchat/crypto-chat for offline checks).
  • Game of 42: the viral growth engine — start solo, find a complementary partner, pay a find-people guide service to recruit missing skills, build to 6 (minimal viable project), then each of the six seeds another six = 7 groups of 6 = 42; nest/bud rather than grow beyond 42; missing roles can be filled by an AI proxy for '42 designers'.
  • Head / Heart / Hands geometry: roles map to card suits (heart = purpose, diamonds = knowledge/head, square/blueprint = hands) plus three mirror roles (architects/systems thinkers, healers, artists/hackers), forming triangle to hexagon to flower to the seats/rooms of the Heart of Gold spaceship, playable as a printed or normal deck of cards.
  • Three bonding curves = guide identity: money (capped/uncapped returns), hours (time bank), and trust/knowledge+promises; a guide private on one axis must be public on another, so it can't be attacked on both at once — linking to the three-graphs-one-identity idea (knowledge graph, delivery/trust-graph blueprint, intent/pledge graph).
  • Agents & ecosystem: Marvin, Eddie the shipboard computer, the Guide to Startups and service guides walk users through the Game of 42; possible single-purpose agents that only add or only spend time; org-level participation (Newspeak House fellows, design schools pledging pupils' hours); precedents/partners include Edgar Cahn's time bank movement (~1,000+ worldwide) and Echo 42's P2P time-banking in Paris/Amsterdam.

Action items

  • Decide to build now and vibe-code a simple Pan Galactic Time Bank plus Game of 42 software internally (mice / audio-or-energy invoicing) before public release; treat it as a milestone.
  • Make onboarding fun and human (audio-note transactions, strong branding, human face for 'no-gen' people) to overcome reticence to log hours.
  • Capture the pledging ceremony (record the pledge, issue credits, signal 'you can pledge again' and invest/donate into guides).
  • Build the skills ontology / offers-and-wants graph and AI matching; auto-identify missing skills and spin up volunteer quests.
  • Design pledge mechanics: pledge bank, repayment, reputation-tied pledge limits, donation vs investment into guides, smart-contract target refunds.
  • Build the Game of 42 interface (complementary-skill matching, head/heart/hands balance, 6-to-42 budding, AI proxies for missing roles); Civilization-style zoomable map of guides/islands.
  • Add a verifier role for time/outcome to deter collusion while keeping the honesty-box ethos.
  • Run the interactive five-year-goal pledging process so volunteers map their own needs and recruit people they will need.
  • Partner outreach: approach Echo 42 (Paris/Amsterdam), Newspeak House, design schools, and the noted US-presidential-candidate time-bank advocate; recruit organisations to pledge.
  • Plan the annual Galactic Meeting with on-stage recognition (towel / 'no kings, we're all kings' sanctification, YouTube-plaque equivalent) and prototype playful surfaces (tabletop card game, Reactable-style musical interface, star-tetrahedron mapping).
