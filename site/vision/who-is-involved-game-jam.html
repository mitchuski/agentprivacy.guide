Who is involved

Active cast. The doers are the makers who show up: "all the game developers that go to that event, the event coordinators, the usual kind of Hitchhiker production community," plus "the hubs and places like that, the hackathon venues," and "the game developers, the game companies, the podcasters and people who are interested in games." The team itself builds and ships: "what we need to give them is a substrate for being able to vibe code these games." Max is named as the source of a specific action: "Max mentioned the original developers of the H2G2 game... to interview them." Mythos agents act here as tools the team deploys: "Marvin AI and the different Hitchhiker characters," the "AI knowledge bases that we've built," dropped in "as NPCs."

Being invited or network activated. The aspirational partner is "the partner called Global Game Jam," with the action to "just get an official partnership." Also pursued: "the original developers of the H2G2 game" for an interview and endorsement ("hey, yeah, let's remake that kind of thing"), "some of the Hitchhiker writers" via "the connection with the writing competition," and "another three gaming companies, an interesting take."
