Who is involved

Active cast. "A grassroots program with a lot of these eco villages" led by "really charismatic, visionary people," who lack tools, so the group works "with them" via "a lot of kind of Zoom calls" and "government liaison." The mice, "the global game jam and the hackathons and the tools we build" produce the IDE.earth interface. Pulmon Verde acts as "a node," "an education node and a wisdom node," registering trees, running walks, issuing credentials to children and tourists. "Some friend of mine who's got an NGO to run global citizen assemblies" who "run one online with like 400 people."

Being invited or network activated. "The United Nations," "model UN" school projects, "Rotary clubs," "the scouts organizations," "model courts," student unions, indigenous people, and "Nameless Ones grandma group, you know, like 50,000 grandmas." Aspirational: "every student union on the planet, every university to have a gathering," "making a call for representatives."
