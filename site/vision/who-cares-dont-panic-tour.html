Who cares

Constituencies. Beyond "everyone else who we've already said cares during our declaration" who "like to see the momentum," this event deliberately scopes to "largely students," specifically "students living in halls, who are able to go to their student unions" and "throw a small event that's connected to the decentralized festival." Also "artists, artists who make either music" or are "familiar with Burning Man" and want a "credentials system" so work is not "burnt" with "no memory." Plus "vibe coders"; students in "politics, art theory, journalism or law" who "just tried chapter GPT" but "don't know about agentic development"; people "worried about the future of AI"; anyone who "loves hitchhikers" or wants the Marvin "token launch"; and a "niche community" into "the future of podcasting."

Nuance or tension. Surprising inversion: the guide communities "care indirectly," reaching outward to "the people who care about that subject." The audience widens from prior events' top-heavy base toward young, term-time students.
