Strategy session shaping the Don't Panic tour — the September outreach campaign launching guides, Marvin, and Hitchhiker Radio toward the November Don't Panic Festival.

A strategy conversation defining the "Don't Panic tour" — the project's outreach campaign that should begin in September at the start of the university term, be announced at Burning Man, and lead toward the Don't Panic Festival in November. The session works through a set of framing questions (who cares, who's involved, what has changed, what proof shows it's working, what makes it feel real) for the Hitchhiker / Game of 42 project. The tour bundles several launches together: the first wave of guides, the Marvin PR push and token-launch tease, a call-out to vibe coders, and the launch of a 24/7 Hitchhiker Radio station. A central audience is students living in halls who can run small events through their student unions, alongside artists, Burning Man builders, vibe coders, and people interested in the future of AI, radio and podcasting. The team stresses that credentialed work, music played on the radio, and guide membership all feed the trust graph, and that the Game of 42's diversity rules (especially intergenerational pairing between H2G2 old-timers and young students) are baked into the policy of forming any assembly.

Key points

  • The tour's purpose: Don't Panic tour starts September (term time), announced at Burning Man, pointing toward the November Don't Panic Festival; it launches the first guides, the Marvin PR tour, a vibe-coding call-out, and Hitchhiker Radio.
  • Who cares: students in halls who can throw small student-union events tied to the decentralised festival; artists/musicians and Burning Man builders who want a credentials system so their work isn't burnt and forgotten; vibe coders; students in politics, art theory, journalism or law who've tried ChatGPT but not agentic dev; and people worried about the future of AI.
  • Early guides: pilot guides built with real communities (e.g. a class Andrea is running, ~15 students, on themes like Guide to the Future of Education or Decentralist Governance); the team picks the guides with the most energy, and those communities reach out to people who care about the subject.
  • Marvin: two versions — foundational Marvin (the model) and the Marvelette, a baby Marvin that can run on a Raspberry Pi (potential Raspberry Pi Foundation angle). A casting call is floated: be the voice of Marvin, write its training data, or use Marvin as a critical/humorous voice for incubators, not-for-profits or courses.
  • Frontier-provider question: open decision on whether to partner with a frontier provider (e.g. xAI/Grok, Gemini 3) versus going pure local first with figures like the Stability AI founder and Audrey Tang plus a sovereign data centre; Claude is noted as already sponsored for Andrea's students. The call order depends on brand strategy.
  • What has changed: fresh young, idealistic energy joins a currently top-heavy base (H2G2 Hitchhikers, Ethereum/open-source OGs); software is now built by external people rather than only the internal mice; and Hitchhiker Radio goes 24/7.
  • Game of 42 diversity policy: the rules of 42 require assembly diversity (age, religion, sexuality, neurotype) so groups can represent everyone for the unsingularity; H2G2 members joining must recruit young people (and vice versa) — intergenerational mentor/reverse-mentor pairing is built into the mechanic, like knocking on neighbours' doors before a party. Missing slots show the community where to focus.
  • Hitchhiker Radio: agentic-first, 24/7 — voice samples woven by AI into an ambient soundtrack over an open-source archive, with real DJs/live shows onboarded over time; every track is credentialed/fingerprinted so artists can share that they've been played, the trust graph at work.
  • Proof it's working: radio is live; 42 seats keep getting filled; the tour visibly signs up universities and locations; Marvin proliferation via an artistic demo/story and a teased November token sale (with dynamic equity for builders) rather than a finished model.

Action items

  • Launch the Don't Panic tour in September at the start of term; announce it at Burning Man and aim it toward the November Don't Panic Festival.
  • Lead the manifesto/declaration and marketing toward the November event and recruit organisers at the start of term.
  • Run a Marvin casting call (voice of Marvin, training-data writers, critical-voice testers) and stand up an artistic Marvin demo/story.
  • Tease the Marvin token sale (~November) with an invitation to build it and earn dynamic equity, ensuring the launch is confirmed.
  • Decide the AI-provider strategy and priority call order (frontier partner such as xAI/Gemini vs local-first with Stability AI founder, Audrey Tang and a sovereign data centre); explore tokens/credits for universities.
  • Approach the Raspberry Pi Foundation about the Marvelette tiny educational agents.
  • Make intergenerational pairing an explicit Game-of-42 policy: invite former H2G2 community members as mentors who must bring in young people (and vice versa).
  • Recruit art students, musicians and coders to vibe-code the radio station; get it good enough to grow by September (24/7 agentic-first, with credentialed/fingerprinted tracks).
  • Book and run real shows/gigs at universities (e.g. Loughborough plus several others) and keep the tour going; bring in hitchhikers such as Oscar.
  • Get Hitchhiker Radio live as direct proof, and keep filling the 42 seats / onboarding universities and locations on the tour.
