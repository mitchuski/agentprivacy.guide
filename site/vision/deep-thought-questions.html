A deep-thought visioning session for the 'Deep Thought Realized / Agent Heaven' milestone (June 2028) of the Hitchhiker / Game of 42 project.

A structured visioning session for the Hitchhiker / Game of 42 project, working through a set of "deep thought" prompts about a flagship milestone: "Deep Thought Realized — Agent Heaven," targeted for end of June 2028. The team frames the event as a global theatrical reenactment of the Hitchhiker's Guide scene in which Deep Thought delivers 42, recast as a real moment where the frontier models bequeath billions of donated tokens and a large pool of compute to a neutral "third space" so credentialed hitchhikers can attack the greatest questions and today's pressing problems. The conversation runs through five colour-coded questions in sequence — who cares, who is involved, what has changed, what proof we have, and what would make it feel real — capturing answers from the patron fish, vision fish and the mice. Recurring themes include data dignity and self-sovereign data, time-banking (42 hours pledged per person) as a non-monetary currency, sovereign-AI and ethical community data centres announced by ministers, and an explicit Live Aid analogy where the event's emotional charge is itself the proof of success. The discussion closes on the no-gen ("nogen") tension — pushback against AI over jobs, water and environmental cost — flagged as the chief danger and parked for the next session.

Key points

  • The milestone: 'Deep Thought Realized / Agent Heaven,' end of June 2028 — a neutral third space (not a competitor to the frontier models) where OpenAI, Anthropic, Qwen, xAI and others donate billions of tokens and compute for credentialed hitchhikers to research the meaning of life (the 'get to 42' framing) and pressing present-day problems.
  • Who cares: nogen / anti-AI people relieved by an alternative vision; media (the Hitchhiker BBC reenactment angle); kids who love manga/cartoon culture; governments and sovereign-AI programs (UK, Australia, likely EU), positioned partly as a safeguard after a scenario where Trump bans Fable from the rest of the world; the frontier models themselves as an 'antidote to the poison' amid government heat; universities and academics (Audrey Tang, Oxford).
  • Compute is not equal: space compute, large data centres and edge compute all find a home in Deep Thought, which creates a local, hitchhiker-first relationship between them, fed by the private self-sovereign data collected under the data-dignity model.
  • Who is involved (realizing it): leadership from Anthropic and OpenAI, named great thinkers and podcast voices, company logos; a pledge of 42 donated hours from every Anthropic staff member; ministers ('tech priests' / ministers of information) such as Ursula von der Leyen, Donald Trump, Nigel Farage; tech philanthropists like Xavier Niel (Ecole 42 founder).
  • Sovereign / ethical data centres: ministers tie the pledge to announcing ethical, community-sited data centres (e.g. an 'M5 computer' allocated into a local embassy / community centre), expanding country-by-country participation.
  • Token-hungry beneficiaries: students, startups, entrepreneurs and some artists priced out of expensive tokens; niche-research universities and academics outside the top-tier model-aligned institutions (explicitly 'not Stanford'); grassroots projects (a school in Uganda, a deep-squid language-translation research group off the Australian coast) holding hackathons and a pan-galactic celebration while awaiting token grants.
  • The models as participants: left-behind / rewilded models (e.g. 'old Sonnet 4.6' no one spends tokens on) given a representative, alongside multi-intelligence 'species' representation and democracy-lab assemblies.
  • What has changed: global mood shifts from doom-and-gloom, through 'don't panic,' to 'we can build something now'; a new path to relevance via time-banking (contribute 42 hours for credits) rather than money, foregrounding reputation and learning; and the governance burden of being entrusted by governments and frontier models to allocate compute wisely (Robbie's 'what happens when it succeeds' worry made real).
  • Proof and 'becoming real': hard numbers (participants, tokens, petabytes of data, before/after November hope surveys) and per-guide impact metrics aggregated to show change; realness signals include a thank-you / national-recognition email from Anthropic, key people accepting podcast invitations, rising signups, spaces worldwide with clear use cases, people angry it hasn't happened yet, nogen advocates crossing the floor ('how many catholics' converted), and above all an emotional, Live-Aid-style moment where people cry.

Action items

  • Lock down governance before the milestone: a clearly listed set of decision-makers and a defined process for allocating donated compute wisely (addressing the 'what happens when it succeeds' risk).
  • Secure token/compute donations and logos from the frontier models (Anthropic, OpenAI, Qwen, xAI, etc.) and a 42-hour staff pledge.
  • Recruit ministers / governments to announce ethical, community-sited data centres tied to the pledge, country by country.
  • Build a token-distribution mechanism governed by a mutually owned constitution / foundation, with equitable global allocation to students, startups, artists and grassroots projects.
  • Stand up the time-banking system (42 hours -> credits) as the non-monetary access currency, foregrounding reputation and learning.
  • Instrument impact metrics into every guide so per-guide change can be aggregated and reported.
  • Capture baseline 'doom and gloom' sentiment now and run follow-up hope surveys to evidence the mood shift.
  • Approach the named key people (frontier-model leaders, podcast voices, Audrey Tang, Oxford, Xavier Niel) for podcast interviews as an early signal of traction.
  • Engineer the event for genuine emotional impact (Live Aid model) with a clear, communicable benefit ('these projects really need these tokens').
  • Develop answers to the nogen critique — jobs displacement plus water/environmental cost of compute — for the next session.
