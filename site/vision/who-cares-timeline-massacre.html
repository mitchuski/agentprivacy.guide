Who cares

Constituencies. This event is inward-facing: the people who care are the team itself, the planning group around the "Hitchhiker / Game of 42" roadmap. They speak as the doers but feel the stakes as believers; one says "i would feel pretty special if the mouse house managed to pull that off," naming the mouse house as the moved party. Downstream beneficiaries are named but not present: "all humans" (the "4.2 million tokens for all humans"), students signed up at events, hitchhiker members who would gain "access to this specific model that is like the hitchhiker fable," climate refugees tied to the embassy and "hitchhiker passport" idea, and people in the "housing crisis" the social-housing stretch goal targets. Foundation-model providers ("e.g. anthropic") are courted, not served.

Nuance or tension. The surprise inversion: the frontier models are reframed from adversaries to partners, "we're not at war against them," reaching "peace." The audience scope here is deliberately narrow and internal, board pruning, not public.
