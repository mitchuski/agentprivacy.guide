How it 'becomes real'

Asked directly. Asked what would make it feel real, the answer is small and almost shrugged off: "that's kind of a no-brainer it just send it out feels it's going to become real i don't know what else to say to me well it's just yeah step one." Another agrees: "yeah yeah yeah i agree." The session then closes immediately ("should we have a coffee break"). The felt-realness here is modest and undramatized, located in the simple act of sending rather than in any swell of feeling.

Texture from elsewhere. The realer texture surfaces under "what has changed": vision fish "coming in which will... make it feel real," and the "unblocking of hesitancy and uncertainty" once "we'll have... launched the ship out robbie will be on a journey." One speaker feels "more emboldened and unblocked," able after this "moment" to say "i'll be the guide to privacy." Realness is felt as permission to speak plainly, not as spectacle.
