What changes

Stated changes. "We've actually... expanded the network of who we are out to outside of just the core team," with "some vision fish coming in which will... make it feel real." "For the first time a presence on social media," press releases "sent out," and "the 42 events hopefully we'll start booking those" plus arranging podcast guests and being invited to participate. One speaker names "an unblocking of hesitancy and uncertainty," able to say "have a look at the website" rather than teasing. "The world will start taking this project seriously." The mice's relationship "has been made public," now "associated to... robbie and the hitchhiker community," "almost like an inclusion."

Hedged or possible. "We may have some impersonators." "It could it has the potential to have like moments of virality as it gets discovered by different algorithms," so "we're present in many places that we're not aware of," like the unknown Turkey H2G2 spin-off. "I think that after the declaration comes out people will make themselves aware to us... which will be a change." The escalation is contained: bigger, bolder moves stay staged for later.
