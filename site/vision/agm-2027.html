Strategy conversation framing the first Annual Galactic Meeting (AGM) for the Hitchhiker / Game of 42 project — awards night, Improbable Lottery launch, and burn night.

A strategy conversation framing the first-ever Annual Galactic Meeting (AGM) for the Hitchhiker / Game of 42 project, imagined for July 2027. The pair work through the AGM as part awards night, part future-casting night, and part "weird party" held across 42 venues worldwide — even if some venues are just someone's bedroom — timed for the summer festival circuit after universities break up. The central deliverable is launching the Improbable Lottery, with a realistic aim of six 42k prizes (one guaranteed) for the year's core group of "mice" and Hitchhiker fellows, funded via a "42 times 42 minimal patron fish" model. The AGM is positioned as the moment the project gets financially serious: dynamic equity contributions are locked in ("burn night"), IP is expected to happen, funding pledges from foundations (Ethereum Foundation, Solana Foundation) could be announced as peer-pressure moments, and the community gets a disclosure of past happenings and forward plans. Throughout, the tone is a humorous, Towel-Day / "Hitchhiker's Christmas" celebration, with inspirations like Australia's Triple J Hottest 100 parties and Spotify Wrapped feeding the idea of aggregating highlights from people's trust graphs.

Key points

  • First AGM (July 2027): the first Annual Galactic Meeting for all hitchhikers; framed as awards night plus future-casting night, congratulating anyone who has contributed and exciting people about what comes next.
  • Improbable Lottery launch: the headline announcement; at least one guaranteed prize (e.g. a 42k reward, golden bath, or trip around the world), aiming for six 42k prizes for the year's core mice/Hitchhiker fellows, with 42 as the ambitious stretch.
  • Funding model & announcements: '42 times 42 minimal patron fish' makes six prizes reasonable; financial info and funding announcements live here, including foundation pledges (Ethereum Foundation, Solana Foundation) as a peer-pressure moment.
  • Burn night / equity lock-in: dynamic equity contributions move from fluid to solid and reliable, fixed and locked for the next year under a new rule system; this is also when IP is expected to happen — a validating, trust-building change.
  • Audience & who cares: all hitchhikers/contributors, plus observers — critics, journalists, the reviewing/assessing/reporting industry, the sceptical and the hopeful, the audience being onboarded, and those anticipating the lottery via the grapevine.
  • Who is involved: mice and fish presenting on stage; contributors coming up to receive a badge; 42 venues with global coverage; any partner; and any author of a (capital-G) Guide.
  • Proof it's working: turnout numbers, lottery success, consensus among members, money actually given to people without feeling extractive, and 42 venues organising for the date.
  • The vibe: a weird, humorous summer party (turn up on a Greek beach with your mates), a 'Devcon moment,' echoes of Triple J's Hottest 100 parties and Spotify Wrapped, distributing towels to all 42 venues — 'Hitchhiker's Christmas.'
  • Pledges & dual prize tracks: a Pledge Day where members pledge their 42 hours for vision-setting; an initial-pledge lottery multiplier; two prize types — pure lottery wins vs. selected bursaries/fellowships — plus improbable gifts (a rose, strange handmade objects).

Action items

  • Confirm AGM date in July 2027 and organise events across the 42 venues (global coverage).
  • Launch the Improbable Lottery; guarantee at least one prize ticket and aim for six 42k prizes.
  • Validate the '42 x 42 minimal patron fish' funding model to underwrite the six prizes.
  • Secure and announce foundation funding pledges (e.g. Ethereum Foundation, Solana Foundation).
  • Plan the 'burn night' mechanics to lock in dynamic equity and the new annual rule system.
  • Investigate legal feasibility of running the lottery across jurisdictions (UK confirmed doable; global is the stretch goal).
  • Make attendance a condition of membership / lottery eligibility to help with ticketing and avoid civil/legal issues.
  • Aggregate Hottest-100-style highlights from members' trust graphs (with their presence and permission).
  • Distribute towels to all 42 venues as the physical Towel-Day ritual.
  • Stand up a Pledge Day with 42-hour pledges, an initial-pledge lottery multiplier, and balance lottery wins against selected bursaries/fellowships.
