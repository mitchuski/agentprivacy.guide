Who cares

Constituencies. The deliberate scope flips toward institutional money. As one speaker opens, "we're flipping the script from gamers to institutional davos suit wearing" partners "and us their risk manager people," framed as "the earth os partnerships." Another names the surprise audience: "people who have been observing the hitchhikers from afar being like cutesy interested in a more like curious way but haven't had like the the formal justification to be involved yet" and "this is kind of the venue for them." A third hardens it: "the money people care so this is the event for the patron fish," reaching "family offices uh funds" and "other institutions and partners like the academic ones." Davos-grade big thinkers are also courted.

Nuance or tension. The inversion: an art/gamer project now addressing "davos grade problems like these big thinking people that are like gathered to try and figure shit out." Yet they insist "it's hitchhiker business," not just business. This widens the audience from gamers (earlier events) to suited institutional capital.
