🗝️ Understanding as Key

Four bodies of work converged on the same invariant this cycle: authentication by comprehension, not possession. A key can be stolen, delegated, or minted; understanding has to be held. Each lane proves it differently — a gate, a registry vocabulary, a classroom rite, and a compression discipline — and none of them store the secret.

The four lanes

  1. The gate — Kyra Gate's understanding challenge: concept anchors and a committed proverb; the agent answers in its own words, echoing scores zero, and every attempt is auditable before it is judged.
  2. The registry vocabulary — the understanding trust-task family (offer · exchange · grant · seal): comprehension as a bilateral exchange written to the ToIP trust-task meta-schema.
  3. The classroom rite — the HH Workshop's census gate: a blind Fiat-Shamir draw over 31 canon claims decides which understanding is interrogated; the tongue you didn't polish is the one questioned.
  4. The compression discipline — the understanding-compression skill: one invariant, many tellings, every re-expression proven to compress back to the same core.
