# guide.agentprivacy.ai — the guide door

The one command and its five starting doors live at
https://agentprivacy.org/skill.md and https://agentprivacy.org/begin/#one-command .
"Start at: the guide" means **this host**.

There are two guides; they are not the same thing:

- **This host** — the reference snapshot: forkable wiki pages with source
  lineage, one section per site of the federation. Read-only. No journey is
  recorded here.
- **https://agentprivacy.ai/guide/walk** — the learning walk on the landing
  site, where a source-linked walk can be recorded into a City Key
  (`guide_search → guide_page → guide_neighbours`, see
  https://mages.city/mcp-entry.md ). The harness fleet page is
  https://agentprivacy.ai/guide/the-dual-agent-harness .
