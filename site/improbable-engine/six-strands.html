guide / Improbable Engine / Six Strands

The fixed instrument of the Improbable Engine: six strands winding the torus, one per axis of the 64-vertex sovereignty lattice — the five Lattice Labs plus the knowledge hearth. Whoever runs the engine, these do not change; collaborators bring pages, not axes.

  • Reclaiming the Interface (Olia Lialina) — protection, amber
  • Playful Misuse of everyday software (viola he) — delegation, sapphire
  • Obscure Detritus (Alex Declino) — compute, violet
  • Minor Indexes (selfservice.studio) — memory, rose
  • The knowledge hearth (the federation) — connection, teal
  • Solidarity Economies (Sepp Eckenhaussen and Geert Lovink) — value, emerald

The lab-to-axis seating is conjectural and labelled so in the engine's own data. The count is the load-bearing part: five labs plus the hearth equals the six stratum-1 roots of the lattice. The hearth lands on connection — the network axis — which is also where the research's edge-structure claim lives: the wiki's role at the festival is the bonds, not a sixth node.
