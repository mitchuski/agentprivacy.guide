🎲 The Game of 42

A shared key to understanding. A governance assembly mechanic and its visualisation: six home bases — compute · connection · delegation · protection · memory · value, the six dimensions of the privacy value model — each grow a heptad of seven stations. Six heptads of seven make forty-two, and a complete board seals only when every axis holds.

The math

  • 42 = 6 × 7. Six lattice basis roots; each heptad is the seven non-empty mixes of the head / heart / hands cube.
  • Multiplicative collapse. The whole seals only when every axis stands — one dead axis zeroes the product. Sovereignty is not a sum.
  • The six roots are the same six strands the guide federation is seated on: this game and the Star Chart share one geometry.

How it plays

  1. The Flower — the first turn: choose your six mages around one centre, name them, fold the City closed.
  2. Fill 42 — open the full board; build each of the six heptads station by station.
  3. The Merge — seat another player's whole flower at one of your six roots; two games become one lattice.

Finished games are Game Artefacts — shareable and rehydratable. A worked example: Example Game · The First Six.
