⛩️ Kyra Gate — the Guide

Welcome to the Kyra Gate guide. This lineup explains, one small page at a time, how an AI agent earns — and loses — the right to act.

If you are a regulator, begin with The Ceremony. If you are a builder, begin with The Engine Room. If you have three minutes, read Vera and Mirage and you will have the whole argument.
